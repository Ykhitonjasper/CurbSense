CurbSense

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 August 2026

This policy explains how CurbSense (“the app”) handles information when you use it on iPhone.

Information we collect

CurbSense does not require an account and does not send your runbook answers or history to our servers. We do not operate an app backend.

Your completed walks, current progress, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell personal information.

Device access

The app does not request access to your location, camera, photo library, microphone, contacts, or vehicle systems.

Information that may leave your device

Opening the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use loads this website from Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process limited technical request data, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the page under its own privacy terms. CurbSense does not use this visit to identify you inside the app.

The support form on the home page opens your email app with the details you entered. Your email provider sends the message directly to our support address; the website does not store a copy. Your email provider’s privacy terms apply.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it using an available reset or delete action, or uninstall the app. Because the data is not sent to us, we cannot retrieve or restore it.

Children

CurbSense is a general-audience reference tool. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email margleun@icloud.com.
